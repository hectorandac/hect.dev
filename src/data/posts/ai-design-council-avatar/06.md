My note made the beard the top fix. The critics added jaw close-ups and a beard check: a script, not a critic. Each still is also rendered with flat material colors, so it knows which pixels are skin. It walks the skin row by row from the eye line to the chin and fails on dark steps (for example, a lightness drop of more than 1.5 L\* between rows; L\* runs from 0, black, to 100, white).

<details>
<summary>The exact thresholds</summary>

A still fails the beard check if any of these is true:

- Lightness drops more than 1.5 L\* from one row to the next, or more than 2.5 L\* in total.
- A jaw corner is darker than L\* 85 (80 on the light themes).
- More than 0.5 % of the skin turns cool (bluish or lilac).
- More than 2 % of the skin drifts off-hue.

</details>

By round 6 the skin was clean: all three critics agreed, and the beard check passed 40 of 40 stills.
