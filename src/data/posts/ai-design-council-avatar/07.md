I looked at the same stills at the size she is actually shown. The gray shading was gone. What was left were a few hair cubes: real, but at that size they didn't read as stubble to me. That's a taste call, not a measurement, and I made it against three critics. I shipped round 6.

> "Not perfect" and "not ready" are different calls, and that one was mine.

This is a familiar ML evaluation problem. The critics were LLM-as-judge with a rubric; the beard check was a metric. The metric said pass and the judges said not yet, and each was right about what it measured. I settled it the way you settle an eval disagreement before release: pick the condition that matters, her real size on screen, and look myself.

## Same face, native code: proving the port matches

The floating bubble and the watch already used a separate native OpenGL ES renderer, and it still drew the old blob. After round 6 it was ported to the new model. A rule in the brief made that feasible: every feature had to be buildable from instanced cubes and one shader. The port is generated from exported model data, not redrawn by hand, so she doesn't drift into a second character.
