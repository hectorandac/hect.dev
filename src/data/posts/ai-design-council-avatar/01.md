The first version of Laura's face was my idea, and I didn't like it. I had asked for something specific: "instead of a circle it is a 3d floating character… pixel cubes with a bit of luminance and transparency". What showed up was a translucent aqua and lavender gumdrop, cubes orbiting it, bloom on everything. The idea was right. The look wasn't, and I couldn't say why.

So I set up an AI design council and stayed the one who decides. Six rounds later, in one evening, she had a face.
