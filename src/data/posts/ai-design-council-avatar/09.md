## What didn't work

- **The hero view.** The council's round 6 summary: “No ‘wow’ at hero size yet.” I judged the ship call at her real bubble size, so the large view is still open.
- **The checks.** The beard check sampled only skin, so it passed 40/40 with hair on her jaw. The fix, a `jawring` check for any non-skin pixel on or under the jaw, was planned for the next pass and never built.
- **Merging.** All three lanes edited one renderer file, so merging, not compute, was the bottleneck. More designers or more compute wouldn't have helped.

## What I'd tell another engineer

I'm still not a designer, and I didn't need to become one. Three things carried this:

- **Give critics different lenses, and make them judge the real renderer's output.** Style, UX and concept critics disagree usefully.
- **A check shows what it measures, and its misses need a person.** The beard check passed with hair on the jaw.
- **Keep the ship decision human.** Strict critics will keep finding one more thing. Someone has to decide when it doesn't matter at the size people see.
